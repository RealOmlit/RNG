Your main roll picks a random integer from 0 to 1,000,000, inclusive. Most results display as six digits; 1,000,000 gets a seventh. It locks instantly, then the animation slowly tells you what you got. The last digit takes its time. Use Reveal now to skip, or Less suspense for a quick reveal.

  1. One main roll every 24 hours. No rerolls. No changing your number. This is your ranked result.
  2. Collect 100 badges. Digit patterns, prime numbers, polygonal numbers, and internet references earn Entropy Points. Rarer badges give more EP. All matches stack, and past rolls count toward the expanded cabinet.
  3. Let the score judge you. Your total EP determines your rarity, from Trash to Anomaly.
  4. Quests earn separate bonus rolls. Claim each reward once a UTC day. Bonus rolls never replace your main roll or enter the daily leaderboard, and expire at midnight UTC.

The leaderboard is shared by everyone playing on this server. No skill, money, or “luck boosts” involved.
